# Art direction notes for VRSchool

Specialist assessment prepared October 29, 2026. These are creative recommendations, not measured preferences among players aged 13 to 17 or a popularity ranking.

## Best visual references

**Start with Royale High for the fantasy school setting, Brawl Stars for readable creature silhouettes, and Adopt Me! for an approachable world.** Genshin Impact is a useful aspirational reference for landscape and atmospheric depth. Dress to Impress can inform student expression. Minecraft can inform modular construction and visual consistency.

There is no established VRSchool art style in the recovered materials. The concept documents describe friendly fantasy teachers, a stone and wood campus, a hilltop town, forests, and themed classrooms. I inspected the composite of `assets/source/Untitled-1.psd` without modifying it: it contains an infographic listing AI tools, rather than character or environment concept art. Accordingly, the matches below describe fit to the written concept, not a match to approved artwork.

| Reference | Best contribution | Main adaptation |

## Art director blurbs

### Royale High

This is the closest conceptual environment reference: magical architecture, decorated personal spaces, and costumes make a school feel like somewhere players can belong. For VRSchool, use a recognizable campus outline and one striking landmark per department, then give the faculty their own material families: frosted stone for the Yeti, warm brass and paper for the Dragon, and botanical glass for the Chuul. Keep the magical invitation; develop a broader palette and range of moods so the campus serves more than one taste. [Official Roblox experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/735030788/Royale-High).

### Brawl Stars

This is the strongest character readability reference. Exaggerated shapes, substantial props, and large color blocks help distinguish an ensemble quickly. Give each teacher a unique outer contour, a dominant gesture, and one teaching tool visible at thumbnail scale. The Beholder might use a wide reading lens, while the Ettin carries an oversized hinged atlas; test the silhouettes in monochrome before detail work. Borrow the visual clarity while establishing warm facial performances and patient body language appropriate to mentors. [Official Supercell game page and character artwork](https://supercell.com/en/games/brawlstars/).

### Adopt Me!

Use this as a reference for making unfamiliar creatures welcoming. Rounded volumes, uncomplicated surfaces, and expressive faces offer a useful starting point for a kind spider, mimic, or dragon. VRSchool should add enough asymmetry, academic costume, and material contrast to give these creatures the presence of teachers rather than collectible plush toys. A friendly low contrast campus backdrop can carry bold faculty color accents. The relevant lesson is approachable form and social warmth; creature ownership is unnecessary for the school premise. [Official game site and imagery](https://www.playadopt.me/).

### Genshin Impact

Use the fantasy landscape treatment as an aspiration for composition: distant landmarks, clear skies, lush vegetation, and intentional color separation can make a campus feel larger than its playable footprint. For VRSchool, concentrate that ambition in the arrival view, one courtyard, and a classroom vista. Favor broad value masses and a few memorable architectural motifs. A full anime production style would create a much larger character, animation, and environment workload than the current project warrants. [Official HoYoverse game site](https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/).

### Dress to Impress

The useful visual lesson is how a controlled presentation space makes individual styling legible. Apply that to portraits, drama class performances, club uniforms, and a student showcase with lighting that preserves color and outfit detail. Faculty and students need expression systems that work with different proportions, skin tones, accessibility choices, and nonhuman bodies. Avoid using one fashion body as the visual default for every character. This is an identity reference more than a campus or creature reference. [Official Roblox experience](https://www.roblox.com/games/15101393044/Dress-To-Impress).

### Minecraft

Minecraft demonstrates the visual coherence available from a strict construction vocabulary. VRSchool could use a modular kit of arches, stairs, bookshelves, classroom fixtures, and terrain shapes while reserving unique geometry for teachers and landmarks. Establish a small material library that separates interactable objects from background structures. A voxel treatment is an optional art decision, not a requirement; modular design can support softer stylized forms while still keeping campus expansion manageable. [Official game site and imagery](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us).

## Proposed visual direction to test

Build a stylized fantasy academy with readable monster mentors, warm stone and timber, and subject specific color accents. Use confident shapes and designed costumes to avoid an overly young tone. Keep expressive faces and hospitable classrooms consistent with the original promise of compassionate teachers.

- **Shape:** Give every teacher a distinct silhouette. Limit costume detail that obscures anatomy or signature props. Test a Beholder, a Yeti, and a Mimic together before expanding the roster.
- **Palette:** Use a shared neutral campus foundation, with limited department accents such as amber for language, cyan for mathematics, and green for science. Pair color with symbols and shapes so it is never the sole signal.
- **Materials:** Start with stone, timber, cloth, metal, ice, and plant surfaces. Prefer broad readable roughness and color differences over dense noise. Reserve shine and emission for meaningful magical objects.
- **Mobile:** Test character faces, signs, silhouettes, and interactable objects at actual phone size. Establish environment and shader budgets through profiling on the minimum device before producing the full campus.
- **VR:** Evaluate faculty scale, comfortable personal distance, and readable world text in the headset. Avoid building the visual language around aggressive bloom, constant particle effects, or rapid camera motion. Choose budgets only after the target headset and rendering pipeline are known.

The first art decision should come from a small scene test with intended players: compare a Royale High inspired environment treatment plus Brawl Stars inspired silhouette clarity against a softer Adopt Me! inspired treatment. Use original shapes, characters, and compositions. Evaluate perceived age fit, warmth, teacher recognition, and whether students want to spend time in the room.
