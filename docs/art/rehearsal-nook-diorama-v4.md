# Rehearsal nook — isolated corner reference

The latest reference reorganizes the selected window rehearsal nook as a complete cutaway module for 3D-generation input. It uses two perpendicular walls, a visible floor slab, open front sides and no roof.

Selected image: [Rehearsal nook diorama](../../assets/concepts/2026-10-30/environments/04-rehearsal-nook-diorama-v4.png).

## Composition review

- The broad piano provides a left-hand anchor; the taller window and long cushioned seat balance it on the right.
- The lower central music stand and stool lead into the scene without hiding the piano keyboard or most of the window seat.
- The cello, case and basket form a contained secondary group at the right edge; hanging lute and plants add height above the piano.
- All major objects are grounded within the slab. The exposed floor perimeter and open front make the module boundary readable.
- This is a visual composition check. Exact dimensions, hidden surfaces and collision clearance remain to be resolved when constructing the model.

## Drawing treatment

Use expressive graphite/plum linework with varied contour weights and quieter transparent washes. Surface decoration must not become uniform distressed noise. This direction is recorded in [the art direction guide](concept-direction.md) for subsequent drawings.
