The Unity sheet is generated look-development art, not an implemented Unity scene or evidence of headset performance. Its lighting direction interprets Meta's in-device home/lobby as broad soft daylight, warm interior fill and accent lamps, readable shadows and distant mountain atmosphere. Visual research: [Meta Horizon Home](https://about.fb.com/news/2022/06/making-vr-more-social-with-meta-horizon-home/) and [Immersive Home imagery](https://www.uploadvr.com/meta-quest-v81-ptc-home-overhaul/). These are lighting references rather than a specification of a particular headset lobby.

Room content follows [locations](../locations.md) and the [existing pencil-and-wash direction](concept-direction.md). The monastery conversion and Swiss Alpine setting follow the current request. No faculty characters appear; the environment is the subject.

[Initial generation prompts](environment-concepts-2026-10-30-prompts.json) and [refinement and Unity prompts](environment-concept-refinement-prompts.json) preserve the prompt set for iteration. Selected images are stored at their native generated resolution.
