# VR High School — individual black-and-white character drawings

29 October 2026.

Current faculty identities: **Music / Goblin Bard — male, he/him; Language Arts / Brass Dragon — female, she/her; Physical Education / Gnoll — female, she/her; Art / Oobleck — non-binary, they/them.** The revised drawings retain their existing silhouettes and teaching costumes, with subtle feminine styling. Use the current files below; [earlier versions are archived](../../assets/concepts/2026-10-29/individual-line-drawings/archive/2026-10-29-before-gender-revision/README.md).

**18 separate character images: 13 faculty and 5 players.** Each file contains one full-body character on white paper, without classroom scenery, color washes, labels, or a character-sheet layout.

The main reference is Arthur's [goblin music teacher drawing](../../assets/source/artist-sketches/05-goblin-line-reference.png), supported by his [yeti math teacher drawing](../../assets/source/artist-sketches/06-yeti-line-reference.png). This pass redraws the shapes and line work rather than converting the previous painted sheets to grayscale. It emphasizes grainy pencil contours, varied pressure, broad expressive brows, angular lapels, exaggerated silhouettes, a few construction strokes, and mostly unshaded interiors.

## Review notes

- The goblin and horned yeti use the proportions and face designs in the original drawings as their strongest guide.
- The [current faculty roster](../teachers.md) governs genders and pronouns. Subjects, creature types, and the female lich headmaster are retained. Costumes remain appropriate for a school, village, and city.
- Creature-specific forms remain separate: floating beholder, six-legged displacer beast, eight-legged spider, two-headed ettin, flowing Oobleck, and a chest-bodied mimic.
- The mimic received a targeted correction after its first generation gave it a humanoid torso. The final image returns it to a squat chest body with arms attached to the chest and short feet.
- Intentional exploratory pencil strokes remain. These are concept illustrations, not cleaned vector paths or production model turnarounds.
- Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Earlier geometric and painted sheets remain available in the [concept direction index](concept-direction.md).
