## Direction and continuity

The detailed block treatments were accepted as a suitable level of detail during the conversation. The larger-primitives sheet is an additional option, not a replacement. The user's typo “facial feathers” was interpreted as “facial features”: blank faces with glasses permitted in the simpler version.

Geometric construction takes its cues from the elongated cuboid bodies, rectangular heads, simple joints, and controlled color accents in the user's games. Shape language varies by creature. The beholder floats; the displacer beast is a low six-legged feline; the ettin has two heads; the spider has a broad radial silhouette; Oobleck is an asymmetric slime mass; the mimic is an animated chest. These designs do not require one common skeleton.

The pencil treatment uses the user's own grainy violet line, lively brows and eyelids, exaggerated proportions, and selective color washes. Intentional sketch strokes remain. The recovered PSD is unrelated; the four newly supplied drawings are the actual reference for this direction.

Faculty clothing is civic, scholarly, or practical for the subject. Friendly posture, useful props, and open classrooms communicate the school's tone. Combat armor is unnecessary for this cast. The headmaster remains **female**, following the roster: white bun, spectacles, long teal academic coat, cream dress, burgundy ledger, and brass hands. The brass hands and costume are new concept choices, not facts in the original document.

## Student wardrobe and Ocean handoff

The five player designs are early-level students, approximately 15–17 years old. Their class is suggested by an ordinary school outfit and one useful prop. Each has a different ancestry, height, build, and silhouette.

| Character | Ocean / River garment starting point | Separate fantasy assets or fit work |

| Elf ranger | Henley, cropped trousers, ankle boots; moss and tan | Pointed ears, map and pouch; test long-limbed proportions |

The garment families are grounded in Ocean's **27 October 2026 catalog expansion**, not the older seven-item starter list. The concepts do not imply that fantasy ears, horns, tails, books, instruments, or bags already exist in that catalog. Exact garment cuts, proportions, accessories, and intersections still need a modeling and fit pass. The painted and drawn rows are art-direction comparisons of the same outfits, not separate wardrobe specifications.

## Review criteria

Check every sheet for cast completeness, correct subject labels, readable creature silhouettes, consistent rendering within each direction, differentiation between directions, complete props and limbs, and practical clothing. For the large-primitives option, check every creature for stray eyes, mouths, or facial markings as well as the humanoid faces. Classroom scenery is supporting concept art, not a geometry budget specification.

Final review: all five faculty sheets contain the twelve subjects and headmaster; the comparison sheets contain three headmaster treatments and five students in three treatments. A cleanup generation removed the unwanted creature faces and reduced Oobleck and the mimic on the large-primitives sheet. Final files retain the generator's native 1536 × 1024 resolution; they have not been represented as 4K print masters. The index excludes superseded generation attempts.
